About the Meshverse Uploader app

Meshverse Uploader is a private tool used only by the Meshverse team to publish our own short gaming clips to our own YouTube channel (@meshverse.gaming). Clips come from our own gameplay, from official clipping campaigns, or from streamers who allow clipping. Every clip is reviewed and approved by a person before it is posted.

The app asks Google for one permission only: "Manage your YouTube videos" (youtube.upload), which it uses to upload approved clips to the Meshverse channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else, and it is not offered to other users.
