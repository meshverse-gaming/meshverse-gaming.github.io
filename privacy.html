Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Meshverse channels and the Meshverse Uploader and Meshverse Publisher apps, which the Meshverse team uses to post its own clips to YouTube and Instagram. Contact: meetshekhat1988@gmail.com.

Who uses the apps

The apps are used only by the Meshverse team, on Meshverse's own accounts. They are not offered to the public and do not collect data from other people.

Google user data

Meshverse Uploader signs in to the Meshverse Google account with a single permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos").

  • What we access: only the ability to upload videos to the Meshverse YouTube channel. We do not read your email, contacts, files, watch history or any other Google data.
  • How we use it: to upload clips that a person on the Meshverse team has reviewed and approved, with their title, description and tags.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is stored on our own access-controlled server in a file that only the uploader process can read. It is never shared or sold.
  • Sharing: we do not share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Meshverse Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are made through the YouTube API Services and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta (Instagram / Facebook) data

Meshverse Publisher posts approved clips as Reels to the Meshverse Instagram account through Meta's Instagram API, using a token limited to the Meshverse Page and Instagram account. We do not access messages or any other people's data.

Clip content and creators

Clips are made from our own gameplay, official clipping campaigns, or streams whose owners allow clipping. Short automated speech transcriptions are used to write captions and titles. Creators can ask us to remove or re-credit a clip at the contact email above; we act within 48 hours.

Visitors to this website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Data deletion

To ask us to delete any data related to you, email meetshekhat1988@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
